# fury gang-vocal takes

Seven real gang-vocal takes from the hook of "fury", a released song by Rys Up Audio's founder
Jordan Rys (24.4 s each, 44.1 kHz, mono, 24-bit). They are the "gang" sources of RAB suite S.

| file | part |
|---|---|
| 144_Audio_5.wav, 156_Audio_12.wav, 160_Audio_13.wav, 164_Audio_14.wav | octave-down gang parts |
| 152_Audio_10.wav, 224_Audio_26.wav, 228_Audio_27.wav | unison gang parts |

The takes are unprocessed recordings: they are already tight to the song's lead vocal, which is not
included. In suite S each take is used as its own reference (guide = the take re-voiced, double = the
take cut and slid), so no lead is needed.
